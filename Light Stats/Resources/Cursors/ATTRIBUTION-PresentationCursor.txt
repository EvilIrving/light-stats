Presentation cursor artwork: 钻石动态光标素材包 (project-owner supplied asset pack).
Bundled files are the pack's 逐帧图集 (frame atlases), one per colourway:

    PresentationCursorHolographicSilver.png = 逐帧图集/全息银钻.png
    PresentationCursorIceCrystal.png        = 逐帧图集/冰蓝水晶.png
    PresentationCursorRosePink.png          = 逐帧图集/玫瑰粉钻.png
    PresentationCursorAurora.png            = 逐帧图集/极光彩钻.png
    PresentationCursorObsidian.png          = 逐帧图集/曜石黑钻.png

Contract used by PresentationCursorAtlas / PresentationCursorStyle, taken from the
pack's 动画参数.json and 校验报告.json:

- sprite atlas 768x1024 = 6 columns x 8 rows of 128x128 cells, drawn row-major
  (index -> column = index % 6, row = index / 6), 48 frames total
- 50 ms per frame, 2.4 s per infinite loop
- hotspot inside one 128px cell, measured from the cell's top-left corner
  (the pack's 热点_128像素), per colourway:
    全息银钻 (24.131, 4.109) · 冰蓝水晶 (23.191, 4.0) · 玫瑰粉钻 (23.826, 4.0)
    极光彩钻 (21.224, 4.107) · 曜石黑钻 (20.893, 4.0)

Every colourway's artwork sits inside x 18…109, y 3…124 of its cell, which is why
the settings picker can crop all five to one shared preview box.

The pack's Windows-only .ani/.cur variants and its GIF exports are not bundled: the
atlas is what the app decodes, and the GIFs are ~12x the size for the same frames.

# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

macOS power users, developers, and system administrators. They want system performance visible at a glance without leaving the menu bar. They value efficiency, privacy, and tools that stay out of the way.

## Product Purpose

Light Stats is a macOS menu bar app for real-time system monitoring — CPU, GPU, memory, disk, network, fan speed — plus memory cleanup. This web page is its external presence: landing, privacy policy, and support hub.

## Brand Personality

Clean, minimal, trustworthy. Like a well-made tool: does one thing well, stays unobtrusive. Apple-adjacent confidence without being derivative.

## Anti-references

- Bloated SaaS landing pages with animated counters and gradient heroes
- Dark pattern privacy policies with hidden data collection
- Terminal-green or neon "hacker" tech aesthetics
- Material Design card-heavy layouts with identical icon-title-description grids
- Glassmorphism and decorative blurs

## Design Principles

- **Show, don't sell.** The app's clarity and restraint speak for themselves.
- **Native-first.** The page should feel at home next to macOS — system fonts, natural colors, no web artifice.
- **Trust through transparency.** Every word about privacy and data is concrete and verifiable.
- **Every word earns its place.** No filler, no restated headings, no marketing-speak.

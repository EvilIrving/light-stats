## Light Stats v1.9.2-beta.1

相对 **v1.9.1** 的补丁预发布（Prerelease）。开启「尝鲜（Beta）」更新通道后可收到；正式版用户不会被推送。

### 🔧 改进 / Changed

- 健康分降低系统负载维度权重。macOS LoadAvg 会计入阻塞在 I/O 上的线程，CPU 空闲时也常虚高
- Health score down-weights the load dimension. macOS LoadAvg counts I/O-blocked threads and often looks high while the CPU is idle
- 总览的负载读数改为每核归一化（load ÷ 核心数），着色与健康分同一组阈值
- Overview load readout is per-core (load ÷ cores), colored with the same knees as the health score

## Light Stats 1.9.1

相对 v1.9.0 的正式版。

### 新功能

- 四种外观：经典、黄金时刻、琥珀、墨夜。新安装默认墨夜。设置窗保持系统浅色
- Four themes: Classic, Golden Hour, Amber, Ink Night. New installs use Ink Night. Settings stays a white tool panel
- 滚动反转可作用于触控板和 Magic Mouse；滚轮可关掉加速度并固定每格行数
- Scroll reverse for trackpad and Magic Mouse; optional fixed-line wheel steps
- 弹窗工具栏可开关保持唤醒；接电合盖时可选用虚拟显示器
- Keep-awake in the popover toolbar, with an optional virtual display for clamshell on AC
- 可选显示器硬件亮度、约 48 小时本机性能记录、最近三小时趋势折线
- Optional hardware display brightness, 48-hour local performance logs, and three-hour trend lines
- 设置入口：通用、监控、输入设备、窗口管理、AI 用量、右键菜单
- Settings: General, Monitoring, Input Devices, Window Management, AI Usage, Right-Click Menu

### 修复

- 自然滚动下方向更一致；睡眠唤醒后会恢复滚动接管
- Scroll direction under Natural Scrolling; the scroll tap recovers after sleep
- 更新校验卡死、退出卡死、安装包缺公证票据
- Updates that hung on verify or quit, and installers missing a notarization ticket
- 点空白穿透到后面窗口；取消标题栏手势后仍会吸附
- Clicks through empty popover space; titlebar swipes that snapped after cancel

## Light Stats 1.9.11

这一版修复 Antigravity CLI（`agy`）的用量显示。Google 把 Gemini 的计量从组摘要接口里拿掉了、只留在按模型接口里，于是 Light Stats 之前只读得到 Claude/GPT 一条额度，正在使用的 Gemini 用量永远停在 100% / 5 小时。现在 Light Stats 同时读两个接口，并把 Gemini 与 Claude/GPT 两套**独立额度**分开成两行显示——每行报该池更吃紧的那个窗口，互不折叠。

This release fixes usage tracking for the Antigravity CLI (`agy`). Google stopped reporting Gemini metering in the group-summary endpoint and left it only in the per-model endpoint, so Light Stats previously saw only the Claude/GPT allowance while Gemini usage — the part actually being used — stayed stuck at 100% / 5 hours. Light Stats now reads both endpoints and shows Gemini and Claude/GPT as two separate rows, one per independent pool, each reporting that pool's tighter window.

### 修复

- Antigravity 的 Gemini 用量重新出现在面板上，不再永远停在 100% / 5 小时 / Antigravity's Gemini usage is visible again instead of staying pinned at 100% / 5 hours
- Gemini 与 Claude/GPT 两套独立额度分开成两行显示，各报各的用量与重置时间，不再压成一个数 / The Gemini and Claude/GPT allowances now show as two separate rows with their own usage and reset time, instead of one collapsed number

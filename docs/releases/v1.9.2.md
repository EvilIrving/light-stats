## Light Stats 1.9.2

相对 v1.9.1 的正式版。

### 新功能

- 找到我的鼠标：可录制触发键；双按全屏聚光指针，三按切换常驻演示指针
- Find My Mouse: record your trigger; double-tap spotlights the pointer, triple-tap toggles a presentation halo
- 离线激活码与 Pro 赠送期：正式收费前启动过 App 的用户永久获赠 Pro
- Offline activation codes and a lifetime Pro gift for anyone who launched during the gift period
- 应用内更新读取 Cloudflare R2 渠道标记，GitHub Releases 作为回退
- In-app updates read the Cloudflare R2 channel marker, with GitHub Releases as fallback

### 调整

- 移除电池充电管理及其特权 helper；电量监控保持只读
- Battery charge limiting and its privileged helper are gone; battery monitoring stays read-only
- 健康分降低系统负载维度权重；Codex 显示为 ChatGPT
- Health score down-weights LoadAvg; Codex shows as ChatGPT

### 修复

- 虚拟显示器崩溃；输入设备关闭路径统一
- Virtual display crash; input-device off-path unified
- 显示器 DDC 在总线挂起后停止 I/O；趋势历史可靠恢复
- DDC I/O stops after a hung bus; trend history restores reliably
